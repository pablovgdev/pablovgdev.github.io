Backend for a Services Marketplace App

A marketplace where people post jobs, from hairdressing to plumbing, and hire someone to do them. I built the whole backend for its mobile apps, with an early OpenAI integration.

Client
Vindy
Year
2023
Type
AI product
Role
Backend engineer

Context

Vindy was a Venezuelan startup building a marketplace for everyday services. People post a job, from hairdressing to electrical work or plumbing, and hire someone to do it.

What I built

The entire backend behind its mobile apps, as a REST API, plus an early integration with the OpenAI API.

How I worked

I wrote the backend with tests. The mobile apps depended on that API, and it had to keep changing without breaking them.

Stack

NestJS, TypeScript
The backend and its REST API.
PostgreSQL
The database.
OpenAI API
An early AI integration.
